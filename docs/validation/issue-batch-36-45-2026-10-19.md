# Campaign issues 36–45 and saved-game regressions

Validation date: 2026-10-19. Changes are on top of `e0abbb1e`, alongside the
existing uncommitted Trials work. This is local validation, not a published
release. Tests used Godot 4.7 and the native desktop runtime, with the original
campaign assets and, for the natural-weapon audit, Lost in Astral assets.

## Saved-game portrait

Loaded the supplied archive's `quick.sav`, dated 2026-10-19 00:25:10, rather
than its newer autosave. Its trained hero has body proportions `(0, .55, .9)`;
the other hero has `(.2, .35, .9)`. The first build selects the original thin
face mesh, which is narrower than the medium mesh. This is a mesh-selection
change from training, separate from the earlier viewport/interpolation fixes.

The main hero now keeps the face associated with the chosen prototype. Body
proportions, attributes, NPCs and hired companions are unchanged. This is a
deliberate portrait-identity improvement; the native game also selected faces
from body proportions. The supplied save does not require rewriting.

Rendered the actual quicksave before and after the change, both selected and
deselected. The selected hero's visible head changed from approximately
48 × 78 pixels to 56 × 78 pixels; the other hero stayed 37 × 53 pixels.
`portrait_viewports.gd` also passed with the supplied trained proportions at
1280 × 1024, 720 × 1280, 2560 × 1440 and 1920 × 1080, including repeated
selection changes and fallback creature portraits.

## Captive lizard dialogue

Reproduced the failed second conversation in the original Hadagan camp
sequence. The preceding dialogue moves OLiz approximately 2.466 cm from the
authored display point, exceeding the old 2 cm staging tolerance. The captive
route now permits 5 cm; other dialogue routes retain their original tolerance.
Identity, location, prerequisite topic, cage, ownership and completed-quest
guards remain in force.

The existing `gipat_captive_dialogue.gd` ran the original conversation and
escape scripts through ordinary interaction commands and topic selection:

| Route | Result |

| Hermit first, after the fix | 21 checks, no failures |
| Commander first | 27 checks, no failures |

| Fresh process loading the completed escape | 17 checks, no failures |

## Moving casts, preview and first-person shadows

The paperdoll now updates after the direct-control lower-body composition.
The existing first-person shadow copies already consume that final pose.

`worker_animation_response.gd` ran a rendered client against a separate local
simulation worker, loaded the original opening map, issued movement while
paused, resumed at 1× and 2×, and cast while moving in several directions in
first- and third-person views. All 13 assertions passed:

- Visible movement began 94 ms after resume at 1× and 55 ms at 2× in this run.
- 153 moving preview samples included 118 changing leg poses.
- 459 first-person shadow samples matched the final moving leg pose.
- Maximum measured preview and shadow pose errors were below 0.0011 units.

These are bounded command/pose checks, not a claim of zero network latency or
full visual equivalence with the original game. They do not resolve the earlier
report of performance degradation after minutes of travelling and fighting.
