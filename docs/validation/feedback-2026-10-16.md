## Reports still requiring a reproducible checkpoint

The solo F9 lightning-wall persistence did not reproduce. A real LocalHost
worker cast and quickload removed the old world and bolt and restored an empty
earlier world, including a rendered control. No production fix is claimed for
that report.

The fresh-approach invisibility failure did not reproduce in the tested native
Nalo/guard case. The original base spell has power 50: it subtracts 0.5 from
visual detectability. Duration runes extend time rather than power. It does not
remove hearing, close peripheral detection, life sense or prior enemy memory.
Power runes can suppress ordinary sight completely. The tests verify both the
ordinary and optimized acquisition paths and footstep suspicion with Silent Step.
No gameplay formula was changed to conceal the unresolved report.

Silent Step's original green star is already emitted above the head and is
visible in the wider rendered capture. Native attachment code adds one metre
at selector 2 and that effect adds another 0.7 metres; a close prone view can
exclude the marker from its frame. Its hearing reduction is active in the
tested case. The Nalo screenshot's historical head distortion was not reproduced
as an exact before/after case; the replacement fade was inspected with intact
head geometry on both tested renderers.

These are focused Linux checks and prepared campaign sequences. They do not
constitute a full playthrough or Windows/Android acceptance. Earlier fixture
setup failures and intermediate camera-clearance failures are retained privately
and excluded from the passing receipts.
