# Cursed Lands 1.0.3

This stable release brings the improvements since 1.0.2 and the 1.0.3 previews to Linux, Windows, Android ARM64 and macOS. You need your own Evil Islands or Lost in Astral data. All co-op players must update together: network protocol 13.

- Settings gain Original, Low, Medium and High graphics presets and reliable save/discard controls. The existing X closes settings; there is no extra Back button. First-import artwork and Android settings handling are repaired.
- Third-person attacks retain their stance for repeated attacks. Grass changes remain stable through small camera movements, and pollen rotation uses game time instead of frame rate.
- Cloud sky quality, ground shadows and water reflections have independent controls. High clouds use a tuned 160-step budget, and unused sky/reflection work is skipped.
- Natural terrain uses a simpler shader where the complete sector and its border contain no junction. Water surface queries use native helpers; domain construction and grass interaction avoid repeated interpreted work. OpenGL ground-contact shaders require less cold preparation.
- Running water carries visible current through connected flat reaches of the same river layer before and after slopes, with bank guidance around bends. Still ponds, seas and excluded liquids retain their previous behavior.
- Includes the co-op loading, travel, save, temporary-character, inventory and campaign-script repairs from the previews, plus the owned renderer, animation and shutdown fixes.

Measured High-cloud GPU savings were 11–22% in isolated 4K Linux scenes. These are effect timings, not whole-game FPS or a promise of 60 FPS on every device. The [final validation record](../validation/stable-1.0.3-2026-10-11.json) covers 28 accepted Linux/Retroid runs with no reported failures and records the package hashes. Two test-harness setup failures and their successful replacements are recorded separately. Additional [setting-cost measurements](../validation/settings-cost-remainder-2026-10-11.json) cover water fields, vegetation and ground-contact preparation.

Android defaults to Compatibility rendering. The optional Mobile renderer still has an Adreno 650 water-flicker report; one unexpected device reboot during private renderer QA has no established cause. The original Adreno 610 startup report and CachyOS/RTX 3050 report could not be reproduced on matching hardware. Windows and macOS are cross-built; physical target-OS testing remains limited. macOS uses the stock engine with script fallbacks and is unsigned and not notarized.

Validation uses focused regression, rendering, settings and device checks. Full campaign playthroughs and broad internet co-op validation were not performed for this release. No original game data, player saves or private QA probes are included in the downloads.
