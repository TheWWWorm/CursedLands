# Cursed Lands 1.16.0

This release adds campaign travel and autochess controls, updates combat presentation and scouting, and reduces rendering and simulation work.

- Revisit the **Canyon of Death** and **Wormfaces' Cave** after reaching the **Necromancers' Desert**, retaining quest state and a route back.
- Restore saved gameplay pause and speed, including separate solo simulation. Open the load-game prompt when the last controlled solo party member dies. ([#27](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/27), [#25](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/25))
- Stabilize Low-quality sun shadows, add **Selection glow → Automatic / Off / On**, and emit toad acid from the animated mouth. Automatic selection glow is hidden in direct camera modes. ([#23](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/23), [#22](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/22), [#21](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/21))
- Discover local autochess rooms automatically. Let the online host select shared **1× / 2×** combat speed. Handle simultaneous guest departures without sending roster updates to closed connections.
- Render player names at the HUD's display scale, use the local player's name, and mark active fights with a sword. Keep hovered creature details stable and show the watched player's timer, opponent, result, level and gold. Keep all nine boards positioned correctly in the overview.
- Animate formation placement and movement, stop locomotion at battle end, clear decorative obstacles from both halves of the field, and fit banshee previews to their animated pose.
- Rotate the Trials camera with **Q/E** or a middle-button drag; use **Home** to reset. Savings interest now reaches **five gold per round at fifty saved gold**. New controls and help are translated into English, Russian and German.
- Run desktop online autochess authority in a separate process and distribute independent battle ticks across frames. Skip invisible-board particle drawing and unused voice lookups.
- Reduce scenery, ground-contact, particle and Forward+ terrain-colour sampling work while retaining live lighting, relief, footprints and effects. Windows and Linux templates skip unused secondary sun-shadow samples outside cascade transitions.

Campaign saves, settings, existing solo Trials armies and documented mod interfaces remain supported. Campaign co-op uses protocol **23**; online autochess uses separate protocol **1**. Multiplayer requires matching **1.16.0** builds and compatible original game data. Browser clients require a reachable secure **wss://** endpoint and cannot host. Online play has no late joining, reconnection, host migration or online save/resume. See the [online autochess guide](../autochess-online.md).

Original game data is required and is not included.
