# Cursed Lands 1.37.0

This release keeps creatures' actions across save and load, steadies path-traced colours, adds a faster path-tracing preset and thaws the snow around fires.

- Keep what creatures are doing across save and load. Patrols continue from where they were instead of restarting and drifting out of step with their partners, and a creature pursuing a target keeps attacking after a reload. Returning to a zone keeps patrol progress but does not resume a fight with a party that left. Older saves load as before.
- Add a Performance path-tracing preset, faster than Balanced. It traces one light bounce; indirect light is slightly darker.
- Keep path-traced colours steady while the camera zooms and pans. High views now centre the traced region on the ground being viewed, and panning prepares the next region without waiting for the previous one to retire.
- With Snow deformation on, camp fires and braziers stand in thawed ground that looks like a trampled trail instead of fresh snow.
- Includes the 1.36.1 fix for attack orders on creatures the party has not yet noticed.

Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and browser builds require legally obtained original game data. Existing saves and settings remain compatible. Campaign co-op uses protocol 23; Autochess uses protocol 5. Use matching 1.37.0 builds and campaign data for multiplayer.

Path tracing and frame generation remain experimental. The release includes the standard approved graphics runtimes. Platform coverage and limitations are recorded in the [release validation](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/blob/v1.37.0/docs/validation/release-1.37.0-2026-10-29.json); the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) contains the detailed changes.
