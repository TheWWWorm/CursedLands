# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.6

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It uses the original game data from an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

This release brings a co-op guest's game closer to the host's in Unreal multiplayer sessions.

- A movie or conversation already running when a guest joins is replayed to it. Guests see the host's credits, scenery effects, wind, grass, wildlife, weather, quest lights, water levels, spell casts and script effects and sounds. Conversation topics use the guest's language, and a conversation no longer reveals units to guests.
- A guest's progress is sent before saves and when the host leaves; a disconnected guest gets no story credit until it returns. Revival progress and each player's round trip reach the guests, and a fallen multiplayer (LMP) guest holds the host in the quest.
- Levers, scenery added by scripts, quest overlays and moved objects such as the Catacombs lift are shown to guests as the host has them. A guest can use levers and steal with its own hero.
- A guest's Original camera takes its pan keys, edges and drag, and the camera follows once per request. The guest's minimap marks the units hostile to its own hero. Quick save and load, the text windows and the belt, weapon and spell keys follow the key layout, and changed bindings apply at once. A guest's collected items list its own finds.
- The host's party faces show the other players' heroes, and the host no longer selects or orders a guest's hero. A guest's orders show their route dots and destination mark under its own path option.
- A guest's trade, repair and item and spell constructor deals wait for the host's result: the pile clears with the purchase sound only when the deal went through, and a refused offer stays to correct. A Shift-click's whole stack can be bought or sold in one deal.
- A companion is hired by the player who finished its conversation and a tamed animal belongs to its tamer. Each player commands its own; while the player is away the host commands them, and they return when it rejoins. The co-op save keeps them. Only the hirer can dismiss a companion, and each player's party counts its own companions when a village offers a hire. Lost in Astral's Kel stays with the host.
- Briefing quest items, broken gear, recharging belt items, closed shops and the constructor's spell limits follow each player's own bag and characters, and scripted repairs reach every player's gear.
- A guest has the touch action bar (Inventory, Journal, Menu, Select all, Centre, Cancel action, Side quests and Chat); the host's touch bar has Chat in a network game. A guest's gamepad walks or steers its units and turns the camera in the field.
- Online Arena guests follow the host's board phase, damage ledger, missiles in flight and rounds past 30. An eliminated guest keeps watching, and guest placement uses the host's rules.
- Master volume (0 to 100) replaces the "All sound" switch. Fogged units that are far from the party stay hidden and off the minimap. Cursor frames no longer bleed into each other. FSR sharpens upscaled frames, geared figures filter anisotropically, and HD terrain atlases are upscaled. The game keeps running and playing sound in the background.
- Corrected for co-op guests: music, wind and combat music, figures whose parts were replaced, name tag height, repeated loot lines, WASD panning, steering without stopping, and the cursor in direct views.

The single Windows build offers DLSS on supported NVIDIA GPUs and Unreal rendering and AMD FSR where supported. Unreal multiplayer uses co-op protocol 1009 and online Arena protocol 1006. All peers need this experimental version. The Godot browser edition has its own version.
