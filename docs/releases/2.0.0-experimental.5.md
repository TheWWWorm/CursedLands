# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.5

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It uses the original game data from an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- A multiplayer (LMP) quest left unfinished stays open when the party returns to the base: its objectives are no longer tested in the base, so it can still be completed and handed in ([#205](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/205)). The multiplayer character keeps its bag between sessions ([#123](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/123)).
- Multiplayer character creation shows Strength, Dexterity and Height on the figure ([#155](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/155)) and plays a line of the chosen voice ([#154](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/154)); the kit page shows the attributes' help ([#157](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/157)) and lets a known ability be raised ([#158](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/158)). A co-op guest's movement is smoother, and its progress is kept when the host's name contains characters such as `<` or `>`.
- Units appear within the party's sight and life sense, scaled by their posture and spellcasting, instead of the server's much larger send radius ([#128](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/128)); the fog-of-war option's help describes this ([#201](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/201)). Units fade in and out at the edge of the fog ([#172](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/172)), and figures turn smoothly toward their heading ([#151](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/151)).
- 'Reset skills...' is back above the experience line ([#200](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/200)). Bag spells the hero cannot take are drawn red on the skills screen ([#122](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/122)), and a body part's help lists its armour per damage type ([#132](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/132)).
- The item constructor counts leggings as heavy armour ([#138](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/138)), limits "All items" to made weapons and armour ([#136](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/136)), builds a previewed item when it is clicked ([#131](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/131)) and draws the hadagan plate's shoulder pads ([#139](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/139)). Camp rows slide as in the original ([#193](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/193)), and the camp's figures no longer go stale after the window is minimised ([#197](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/197)).
- Village people with a conversation waiting are lit ([#182](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/182)). The journal's collected items list the zone's pickups, and the message log is kept in saves ([#194](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/194)).
- Texture packs keep the natural transitions between ground tiles ([#202](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/202)). With wind in foliage on, firs keep their needles ([#203](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/203)). Snow trails follow the snow deformation option ([#167](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/167)), footprints keep each foot's size ([#166](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/166)), and the land under the moon keeps its colours ([#169](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/169)). The move path dots are hidden behind scenery and the target mark lies on bridge decks ([#129](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/129)).
- A film or the graphics test that is running when a map loads ends with it and no longer takes keys and clicks over the new level ([#199](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/199)); films have a Skip button for touch and mouse. Mods may replace the films ([#141](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/141)).
- Zone-change autosaves take their picture after the world is drawn ([#161](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/161)), and save pictures are stored larger ([#163](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/163)). The graphics-driver prompt is shown once per adapter and driver ([#147](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/147)). The Sound page has an "All sound" switch, and choices with more than three entries open as dropdowns.
- Corrected a range of script, save and travel details found by an audit of the port, including Detect Life reaching creatures, belt items refilled on entering a zone, patrol routes kept across a load, and the game-over notice held while a companion can revive the hero.

The single Windows build offers DLSS on supported NVIDIA GPUs and Unreal rendering and AMD FSR where supported. Unreal multiplayer uses protocol 1008. All peers need this experimental version. The Godot browser edition has its own version.
