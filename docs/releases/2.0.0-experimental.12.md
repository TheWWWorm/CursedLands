# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.12

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It requires an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- The original camera uses the original CD 1.06 release's settings instead of the executable's built-in defaults: a tilt of 30 to 60 degrees (40 to 60 in villages), the disc release's pan, turn, tilt and zoom speeds, and its distance limits, with a view beyond the far limit springing back. New profiles start with the disc release's 1-pixel edge scroll band, 30-pixel selection threshold and keyboard camera power; saved preferences keep their values. Units and map objects are lit with the disc release's lighting coefficient. [#285](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/285)
- The light round the hero has the disc release's colour and 5 m radius. [#280](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/280)
- Quest lights are added over the surface as in the original, so they show on any ground. They no longer flood scenery on wet terrain, and a carried quest light stays above its carrier on every co-op seat. [#283](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/283)
- The speakers keep the talk highlight through a conversation's phrases. [#284](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/284)
- A newly hired mercenary's armour is shown in the camp as it is after deployment, in the host's and a guest's views alike. [#279](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/279)
- The dressing figure opened from the travel map keeps its idle animation, and the character doll stays on its pedestal while it stands still. [#282](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/282) [#276](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/276)
- The network game follows the shared pause and game speed option again. [#281](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/281)
- Co-op host and guest run one shared implementation of misses, spell casts, healing, lasting effects, the fog fade, quest lights and notes, pickup and loot lines, party and camp portraits, portrait cursors, drag selection, village movement, Follow, exits, direct strikes and casts, gamepad steering, name tags, dialogue lips and camera, and the inspector figure. Guests fade units into the fog instead of hiding them at once, a late joiner sees the running spells and hears the scripted music, and talkable characters light up from each player's own topics.
- In co-op, a guest's loading cover rises before its first map build, a location is not shown with frozen heroes before it starts, progress saves no longer stall a guest's game, and a class hero on the campaign purse is kept in the host's save and returns on rejoin. Before a trip only connected players' fallen heroes rise and pay the toll, and a returning party member keeps its recorded health and mana while a fallen hero returns alive. Crashes after loading a save, during Lost in Astral travel with a menu open and at exit are fixed.
- In the network game, a player who takes the quest exit at the base goes into the quest alone, as in the original: the others stay in the base and can follow later, and the quest exit brings a player back into the base. Hand-ins pay connected guests at once, a completed quest keeps the traders' stock, experience is shared by the original's weighting, the open travel map silences the music, and guests check exits with the host's rule. Router detection uses the default gateway, and late lobby directory replies no longer replace newer results.
- Combat and AI follow the original more closely: the hit roll's speed term uses the weapon's weight, fire, acid and lightning protection reduce every hit, effects of one kind stack, units wake in 16 m sectors, chases lead the target, allies re-share hostility on the original's cadence, and the party deploys in a line behind the entrance. A moving human plays its walking or running flinch and keeps moving, a revived unit forgets earlier foes, idle voices finish before they repeat, unarmed clips hide the held weapon, and Clairvoyance shows the units in its circle to the scripts.
- The Lost in Astral campfire, the heat haze behind fires and the ground at dusk match the remake's look. Steep land gets its own side texture, estate roof lighting holds at grazing angles, road paving stays off wall faces, route marks lie on drawn snow and spell lightning bolts stay hidden in the fog. HUD faces no longer bleed into the hair, and the campaign loading art fills widescreen screens.
- DLSS upscales when anti-aliasing is set to Off or FXAA, DLSS frame generation is no longer switched off by a short stall, and Fullscreen uses a borderless window while frame generation is on. The ray tracing memory warning is no longer drawn over the game.
- Map loads share the open game data, saves are written and read faster, the Arena prepares its grid on a worker, and a co-op guest builds the next map while the host loads.
- A save keeps the sandbox toggles it was played with, refuses to load when a saved added-unit file is missing, and restores a side quest's units in their own rows. The latest quest crosses saves and travel, camp items keep each copy's wear, and repair and dismantling find recharged items. Saves in long Windows profile paths, texture reloads and concurrent artwork preparation no longer lose saves or stall startup.
- In-game mod options show and edit the running game's values at camp, a mod with a missing texture override keeps its other textures, game files are found whatever the case of their folder names, and a refused manual folder no longer replaces the open game data. Imported Godot saves keep scripted effects, running timed spells and the Lost in Astral clerk's and network heroes' state.
- Companions park with the party that hired them, leave through their dismissal topic and replace their home village's copy on arrival. Lost in Astral paid offers follow the buyer's own purse.
- Autochess markers take clicks only where they are drawn, so every unit can be moved; the landing, inspector and tooltips open at their final size, the shop fits a narrow window, moved pieces are released, and an eliminated player can watch the others. Round 100 ends the tournament, the shop pool holds during a fight, the Wisp glows in shop portraits, elemental charges leave the casting hand and Lost in Astral commanders show their detailed faces.
- Many smaller fixes to sound, music, the console, the message log, the tutorial guide, the objectives, the wiki and translated options.

Unreal campaign co-op uses protocol **1037**. Online Arena uses protocol **1008**. All Unreal peers need this version. The Godot browser edition is version **1.38.0**.
