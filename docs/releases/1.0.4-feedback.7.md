# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Feedback 7

Includes the changes in Feedback 6 and centres the first-person body beneath
the stable camera:

- Align the rendered torso, arms and weapon laterally after the final animation
  pose, including idle sway, movement and attacks.
- Preserve steady camera positioning, vertical body motion and the closed collar.
- Restore original render transforms when leaving first person. Animation bones,
  gameplay geometry, shared materials and other characters keep their original
  positions and appearance.

Existing saves and installed texture packs remain supported. Protocol remains
21; co-op peers should use the same build.

Validation is recorded in
`docs/validation/first-person-alignment-2026-10-12.json`.
