# Cursed Lands 1.11.0

Trials now offers Expedition and Autochess. Autochess puts one player and eight
computer opponents on nine live boards, with creep rounds, random player
matchups, shared preparation and elimination. Opponents are named before combat;
select a name to watch that board, or open the 3×3 overview.

Recruitment grows from twelve to twenty-four families. Twelve families can appear
in the opening shop, with melee and ranged choices in each opening. New recruits
include skeletons, wisps, tigers, banshees, lizards, orc shamans, evil eyes,
armadillos, woodsfolk archers, ogres, red harpies and wormheads. Prices, evolutions
and later expedition reinforcements account for the wider pool.

The HUD scales with display resolution. Snow-facing labels use near-black regular
text; shop metadata, reserves, tribes and player rows remain transparent. The
watched player has a visible marker, and the clock pulses during the last ten
seconds. Bottom main-menu buttons match the campaign buttons in height.

Battle effects stay on their own board and finish after combat. Spectating plays
the viewed board’s battle audio. Opponent models prepare over several frames
before all boards begin together. Combat ends after two minutes or ten seconds
without damage; Autochess draws cost both players ten health.

New recruits fill free field positions, then reserves. Speed is remembered across
new runs and reloads through a 1×/2× toggle. Evolution notices sit below the tribes,
menu icons are centered, and reserve slots fit without horizontal scrolling.
