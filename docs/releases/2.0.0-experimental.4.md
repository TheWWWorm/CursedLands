# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.4

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It uses the original game data from an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- Multiplayer (LMP) traders use the network game's shop records and sections ([#188](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/188), [#192](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/192)). A quest taken in LMP shows on its own map in the quest screen ([#189](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/189)), and a speaker's conversations are sorted as in the original's network game ([#190](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/190)).
- The session chat closes when the game takes the keys ([#195](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/195)) and stays under the journal and the quest screen ([#191](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/191)). The menu theme plays in a multiplayer lobby ([#187](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/187)).
- The multiplayer character screens repeat a held list arrow ([#186](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/186)), and the character figure stands in the middle of its pedestal and turns about itself ([#133](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/133)).
- In the first-person views a click swings once ([#176](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/176)).
- A conversation's actors turn back to their old facing after walking home ([#183](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/183)), and Esc skips a conversation's walk. Villagers keep their work pose between a script's repeats ([#165](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/165)).
- Saving shows a notice in the middle of the screen ([#168](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/168)). The camp lights only the open mode's button ([#124](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/124)).
- Wind in foliage also applies to the main menu ([#173](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/173)). With water and lava effects off, lava keeps its original look ([#196](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/196)). Under Original lighting, shadows receive the full sky light ([#178](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/178)).
- Footprints point the way the walker faces ([#145](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/145)), and the party retries a route blocked by a standing unit on a fresh order.
- Crash reports attach the closed session's log, packaged builds no longer show the engine's ray tracing budget warning, choosing FSR frame generation says a restart is needed, and the Lighting row marks its path-traced steps.

The single Windows build offers DLSS on supported NVIDIA GPUs and Unreal rendering and AMD FSR where supported. Unreal multiplayer uses protocol 1007. All peers need this experimental version. The Godot browser edition has its own version.
