# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.9

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It requires an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- Co-op host and guest run one shared implementation of the field and gamepad controls, including selection keys, Follow, Use/Steal, belt casts, weapon shortcuts, lever use and the camera. Both seats now refuse and accept the same keys in the same situations.
- In co-op, provoking a character makes it hostile only to the player who provoked it and that player's party. Party calls for help, attack-move waits and follower collision waits stay within each player's own party, and unrelated nearby units no longer keep a failed move retrying. Scripted hostility checks, faction diplomacy and the minimap's hostile and Curse markers follow the same rules.
- When a co-op hero falls, a living companion keeps its field equipment, belt, inventory and Use/Steal and can revive the hero. Inventory opens on a living member of the player's own party.
- In the original multiplayer mode, a player's private base and its inventory are kept until the return to it succeeds. Worn equipment and unequipped purchases are carried back, repairs are charged, departure tolls are shared, and a player joining an active quest receives its current state. Script queries for party members and sight answer for the requested player.
- Lost in Astral chests check the lock skill and show its hint. Co-op guests see the Lost in Astral camp fire, their party's roster portraits and the inner surfaces of armour under path tracing. Character and equipment previews are lit under path tracing, and saved quest markers return after loading a campaign save in the Windows package.
- Online Autochess guests see and hear the host's whole fight, including spells, hits, item effects, lightning, blood marks and fighters spawned during the fight. Guest shop sounds follow the host's answer, and the guest hears its fighters' steps and swings. An online game no longer freezes for several seconds while its courtyards load, and the actions and round badge share one row.
- Returning to the main menu and switching between the base game and Lost in Astral reuse the prepared menu island after its first load.

Unreal campaign co-op uses protocol **1032**. Online Arena uses protocol **1007**. All Unreal peers need this version. The Godot browser edition is version **1.38.0**.
