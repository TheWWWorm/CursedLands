# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.8

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It requires an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- Co-op guests use the same campaign rules as the host for camera and gamepad input, selected-party orders, field targeting and revival. Guest units show the host's movement gait and route markers along the full path.
- Sharper artwork can be prepared from the main menu and reused across sessions. Preparation falls back to the CPU when the GPU path is unavailable. Terrain transitions, high-resolution atlas seams, sand ripples and character lighting have also been updated. [#219](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/219) [#223](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/223) [#225](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/225)
- The first game window shows the branded cover, and opening films play while shaders prepare. Zone loads preserve the authored sizes of scenery textures from packs. [#148](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/148) [#127](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/127)
- Autochess keeps its interface steady during character selection, shopping and ready changes. The selected hero carries into the online lobby, equipment selection and rewards are preserved, and fighters resume pursuit after defeating a target.
- Mod imports and catalog downloads continue in the background. Enabled mods can supply startup and loading films. [#215](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/215) [#216](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/216) [#217](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/217)
- Village NPCs stay highlighted while their dialogue topics are open, and movement clicks pass through tree crowns to the ground or bridge below. [#224](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/224) [#221](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/221)
- Tactical pause preserves map ambience. Footprints follow reverse and sideways movement. Eligible hits play damage reactions and voices, Healing particles sit at the caster's and recipient's feet, and the item constructor shows the clickable preview separately from its slots. [#222](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/222) [#218](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/218) [#175](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/175) [#131](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/131)

Unreal campaign co-op uses protocol **1031**. Online Arena uses protocol **1006**. All Unreal peers need this version. The Godot browser edition is version **1.38.0**.
