# Cursed Lands 1.19.0

This stable release adds shared co-op control permissions, camera and character
interface options, and creature filtering on in-game wiki maps. It also improves
campaign navigation and presentation, Trials feedback, and desktop session cleanup.

## Co-op and controls

- Let hosts grant guests pause, speed, quicksave and quickload permissions.
  Configure defaults in the multiplayer menu and individual grants in Players;
  permissions are retained in campaign saves. Guests use F5/F8 or the Save/Load
  menu to operate the host's quicksave slot. ([#50](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/50))
- Separate horizontal and vertical mouse sensitivity in the classic camera.
  Let the modern camera scroll at screen edges over interactive HUD controls.
  ([#50](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/50))
- Remember campaign speed between zones, use normal speed in villages and on
  the route map, and restore the selected speed when returning to the field.

## Campaign and interface

- Add an enabled-by-default option for buff icons with remaining seconds,
  stacked vertically at the top right of the character's general view.
- Filter in-game wiki map creatures by localized name. Following a creature's
  map link enables creature markers and highlights its positions.
- Let patrols lose escaped opponents and investigate their last known location.
  Complete the Suslanger gate escape after the living party gets clear.
- Alert nearby allies on first sight of an enemy.
  ([#49](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/49))
- Make visible witnesses react to an assault, preserve running in aimed combat
  orders, use casting-specific stamina acknowledgements, and finish the final
  partial stride at the character's approach speed.
  ([#48](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/48))
- Apply modern water rendering to lion basins, retain the protagonist's portrait
  face proportions on the world figure, and stage dialogue cameras from the
  destination ground height.
- Retain unaffected navigation blocks when doors open or close.
- Expire cancelled native tooltip timers in the Windows and Linux engines,
  removing accumulated background scans during play and after returning to menus.

## Trials and Android

- Retain damage totals and winner/loser standings colors through preparation.
  Improve result projectiles, casting attachments and elimination feedback.
- Extend the arena snow, retain footprints within a bounded allocation, and
  refine the default camera and disabled shop actions.
- Enable the optional HD Lands texture download on Android, including localized
  installation memory requirements.

## Compatibility and downloads

- Existing campaign saves, profiles and the documented mod API remain supported.
  New options, choices and help text are available in English, Russian and German.
- Campaign co-op protocol is **23**; Trials protocol is **1**. All participants
  must use **1.19.0**; do not mix game versions.
- Update the complete Windows/Linux executable, PCK and native-library set.
  The tooltip cleanup fix requires the rebuilt executable.
- macOS and browser packages use the official Godot 4.7 templates; Android keeps
  its qualified production engine and existing signing certificate. Those engine
  templates do not include the desktop tooltip patch. The macOS package is unsigned.
- Original game data is required and is not bundled.
