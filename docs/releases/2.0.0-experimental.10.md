# Cursed Lands 2.0.0-experimental.10

The experimental Unreal Engine edition is available for Windows x86-64. It requires an existing Evil Islands or Lost in Astral installation.

- Co-op host and guest share the spell keys and spell slots, spell refusals with their unmet requirements, the field cursor, the belt and its direct-view aim, the aim wheel, gamepad buttons, target cycling, minimap marks, journal clicks, item tips and the unit panel. Shift-clicking a unit adds it without reviving, the message log no longer blocks field clicks, and both seats refuse Select all in a network game.
- Maps load faster: terrain, merged scenery, leafy models, collision and static navigation are prepared on worker threads, and texture upscaling at load takes less time.
- Big co-op fights send more current unit poses to guests when the host's frame rate drops.
- The options menu again offers the selection marker under selected units and the original's hold-to-aim for aimed strikes. [#244](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/244) [#245](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/245)
- A stance ordered and taken back during pause is not played, and a stance change no longer interrupts an attack ordered in the same pause. [#256](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/256) [#257](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/257)
- Fearful creatures flee from a sensed hero even when it is behind them. Enemies forget the party when it enters a zone again. [#253](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/253) [#261](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/261)
- A hero who walks up to start a conversation turns to the speaker. Long routes ordered across large maps reach their goal. [#259](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/259) [#260](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/260)
- Rain plays its own sound, and idle heroes say lines that match the shown rain or snow. [#227](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/227) [#233](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/233)
- Water and lava tiles animate as in the original, and the Original preset keeps their waves. Modern lava's crust no longer slides and its lights no longer leave bright spots. [#236](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/236) [#237](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/237)
- Footprints point toe forward, and switching between walking and running no longer leaves extra prints. [#242](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/242) [#240](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/240)
- Standing units pick a new idle animation each cycle, and a village script's last pose is kept until an order. [#232](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/232)
- The camp shows installed spell runes on its spell slots; the in-game spell column no longer draws them. [#239](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/239)
- Mouse look in the first- and third-person views keeps the pointer inside the window. [#238](https://github.com/TheWWWorm/CursedLands/issues/238)
- Village NPCs keep their talk highlight during a conversation. The unit panel's view tabs respond to clicks and play the original's sounds, as does opening the topic list. The menu theme starts over under the Network game and other menu screens.

Unreal campaign co-op uses protocol **1033**. Online Arena uses protocol **1007**. All Unreal peers need this version. The Godot browser edition is version **1.38.0**.
