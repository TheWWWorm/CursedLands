# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Feedback 2

Includes the Feedback 1 fixes for co-op delivery, UI layout and translations,
gameplay settings, sounds, previews, particles, companion experience and water.

- Enchanted weapons, armour and wands recharge at the original rate of 0.1% of
  capacity per 55 ms simulation tick. Depleted equipment in existing saves resumes
  recharging. Each carried copy retains its own charge and durability.
- Automatic equipment spells follow the simulation clock, including pause and
  double speed, and do not consume charge when village spell casting is blocked.
- Enchanted corpse loot retains its spell when moved into the party inventory.

In the original campaign, the ancient helmet casts Eagle Sight periodically and
the ancient sword casts Weaken on hit. Campaign data still defines each item's
spell, capacity, and cost; item stats and spell costs are not replaced by those
from another campaign.

Network protocol 18 and Mod API 1 are unchanged. Earlier campaign saves remain
supported. Linux and Windows packages contain no original game data or saves.
