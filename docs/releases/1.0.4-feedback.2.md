# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Feedback 2

Includes the Feedback 1 fixes for co-op delivery, UI layout and translations,
gameplay settings, sounds, previews, particles, companion experience and water.

- Enchanted weapons, armour and wands recharge at the original rate of 0.1% of
  capacity per 55 ms simulation tick. Depleted equipment in existing saves resumes
  recharging. Each carried copy retains its own charge and durability.
- Automatic equipment spells follow the simulation clock, including pause and
  double speed, and do not consume charge when village spell casting is blocked.
- Enchanted corpse loot retains its spell when moved into the party inventory.
- Regional particle shaders are shared across local effect resets and warmed
  during loading before particles first appear in the scene.
- The destination sky and initial HUD previews prepare their render pipelines
  beneath the loading screen. Charge updates retain existing item models.
- Material preparation includes native vertex layouts and instanced effects.
  Loading remains covered while the enabled scene completes its initial draws.
- Desert dust particles use a smaller size.
- Recruited companions keep their equipment slots linked to the current
  inventory state when their original map names use mixed capitalization.
- Equipment material names accept surrounding whitespace, including the
  Demon's spiked club.
- Holding Alt opens the body-part selection wheel in all three control modes.
  Moving the mouse selects a part; releasing Alt confirms it. Escape and right
  click cancel. With no enemy targeted, the choice applies to the next attack.
- A revived character regains selection and camera focus when it is the
  player's only living controllable character.
- First- and third-person controls register again after zone-loading barriers
  finish. Held keyboard movement resumes on arrival, and the minimap follows
  the controlled character's visible position.
- The original loading-screen letters advance while the simulation service
  builds a map in either campaign.
- Nearby chests and mechanisms are easier to select with the interaction key
  in first- and third-person views. Terrain and reach checks still apply.
- Direct movement can slide around other characters and escape an existing
  overlap. The character's body follows the viewing direction, including while
  strafing, with the same facing replicated to co-op peers.
- The activated tunnel golem receives the original Guard combat motivations.
  Existing saves with the affected activated golem restore those motivations.
- The two Ingos gate guards retain their scripted pose between animation cycles.
- Assembly and disassembly fees are 5% of the completed item's value. Missing
  components still cost their normal purchase price.
- Enchanted item prices include each rune and the original signed base-price
  adjustment. The original green dragon horn sells for 1,000 gold.
- Party snapshots update at up to 20 Hz while NPC snapshots retain their
  existing cadence and interest filtering.

In the original campaign, the ancient helmet casts Eagle Sight periodically and
the ancient sword casts Weaken on hit. Campaign data still defines each item's
spell, capacity, and cost; item stats and spell costs are not replaced by those
from another campaign.

Network protocol 18 and Mod API 1 are unchanged. Earlier campaign saves remain
supported. Linux and Windows packages contain no original game data or saves.
