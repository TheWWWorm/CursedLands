# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Experimental 3

This preview includes the changes from [Experimental 2](1.0.4-experimental.2.md), with save-state, co-op, reflection and health-bar fixes.

- Saving during a paid spell or potion cast preserves the pending effect without charging again. Direct projectiles, committed action state, scripted orders and frozen world time survive reload.
- Partial taming retains its stage, faction history and peace state. Stable pet identities reconnect effects and script references through loading and travel, including dead and looted pets.
- Co-op return saves remap guest actor references and in-flight effects correctly. Lost in Astral's two inspected acid traps register later arrivals through their original script conditions.
- Mirror water on Forward+ renders nearby reflected geometry, including bridge undersides and trees outside the main camera view. The additional view uses half resolution, capped at 1,024 pixels on its longest side, and costs extra GPU time. Natural and other renderers retain screen-space reflections.
- Full-health idle characters no longer show overhead health bars. Enemies fighting the local party retain their bars at full health; damaged characters remain visible.

Packages are available for Linux, Windows, Android ARM64 and macOS, with the asset-free Quick recovery example mod. Bring your own Evil Islands or Lost in Astral data. All co-op players need this release's network protocol **15** because return saves now carry stable actor references and pending paid effects. Mod API **1** is unchanged; see the [mod guide](../mods.md).

The [validation record](../validation/experimental-1.0.4-3-2026-10-11.json) separates source regression checks, packaged checks and platform limits. A combined-effects scene can hang during shutdown; the same shutdown failure was reproduced with the original water code. Full campaign-route and multi-machine acceptance remain open. Windows and macOS are cross-built; macOS is unsigned and not notarized. Android uses Compatibility rendering by default.

Stable **1.0.3** remains available.
