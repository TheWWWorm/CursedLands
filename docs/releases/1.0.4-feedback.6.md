# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Feedback 6

Includes the changes in Feedback 5 and adjusts the first-person body view:

- Position the eye ahead of the collar while retaining the stable posture anchor.
- Show the torso when looking down without cutting away nearby chest geometry.
- Close the hidden neck at the collar, using opaque texture coordinates and
  matching shadow geometry. Keep the original chest and limb vertices.
- Restore the original body mesh when switching to third person. Other
  characters, portraits and gameplay geometry retain their original models.

Existing saves and installed texture packs remain supported. Protocol remains
21; co-op peers should use the same build.

Validation is recorded in
`docs/validation/first-person-torso-2026-10-12.json`.
