# Cursed Lands 1.0.4 Experimental 5

This preview includes [Experimental 4](1.0.4-experimental.4.md) and adds:

- Original transparent rune inscriptions on spell cards, compact rune labels, and descriptions that fit above camp controls.
- Sharper setup-dialog text and Russian/German translations for setup and game rules. Item icons fit their geometry, including offset bows; large creature previews fit their panel.
- The village dragon rests between conversations. Dialogue, movement and combat keep their authored animations.
- Ctrl toggles crouch/sneak in first- and third-person modes, subject to village restrictions. Melee swings reach nearby small ground creatures while retaining range, forward-arc and obstruction checks.
- Scripted point lights display without floating glow discs. Fire and spell lights retain their glow.
- Dismissed companions retain attributes and experience when recruited again, including through replacement map actors. LiA exits with a deployment rectangle can also use it for departure.
- Overlapping NPCs can move apart; equally fast actors meeting head-on choose a deterministic detour.
- Double-clicking a co-op partner portrait follows that actor. In direct control, Tab returns to the controlled character.
- Mirror water excludes light halos, heat distortion and gameplay markers. Missing reflected geometry uses reflected sky, removing hard outlines from the previous screen-space fallback.
- Natural and worn armour share an **80% absorption cap** for physical and spell damage: at least **20%** of positive incoming damage remains.
- **Game rules → Party → Always regenerate stamina** adds optional recovery during movement and combat, at least as large as HP regeneration, including at full health. Higher normal stamina recovery is retained. The setting defaults to Off, is saved and shared by the host, and keeps running/spell costs.

Packages are available for Linux x86-64, Windows x86-64, macOS universal and Android ARM64. The tools download contains the API 1 converter, package builder, guides and asset-free example. Bring your own Evil Islands or Lost in Astral data; no original game data or user saves are included.

All co-op players need network protocol **17** for the shared stamina rule and armour changes. Mod API **1** is unchanged. Earlier campaign saves remain supported; already-lost companion progress cannot be reconstructed without an older save.

Technical qualification is recorded separately in the [validation record](../validation/experimental-1.0.4-5-2026-10-11.json). Windows and macOS are cross-built; macOS is unsigned and not notarized. Mirror geometry uses one selected water level at a time. Full campaign and physical multi-machine testing remain outside this qualification.

Stable **1.0.3** remains available.
